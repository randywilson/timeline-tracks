Timeline Tracks keeps your Google Timeline paths accurate even when you're in the outdoors: hiking, sailing, hang gliding, or anywhere your phone isn't near Wi-Fi access points. It can also help location-sharing and other background location apps in the same way.

It works by requesting a GPS location at a regular interval (you choose how often). A shorter interval is more accurate; a longer interval saves more battery. Every app on your phone that uses location, including Google Timeline, receives those locations automatically. No special integration needed.

Simple by design:
  - Set your interval, from seconds to hours.
  - Tap Start. It runs in the background.
  - Tap Stop, or let it auto-stop when you're stationary.

Privacy: No data is collected, stored, or transmitted. The app does not have internet permission, so it can't send your locations anywhere.

Free and open source at https://github.com/randywilson/timeline-tracks.
